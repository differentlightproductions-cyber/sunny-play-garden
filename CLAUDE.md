# Handoff

Little Sprout Park is a free, static, dependency-free browser games site for a young child (the first player is 3½, a pre-reader who is working on letters). Serve the folder on any static host or open `index.html` through a local server. No build step.

## Layout
- `index.html`, `styles.css`: shell, screens, overlays, all shared styles (game-specific styles are appended in sections).
- `js/core.js`: player storage (`SPG.store`, localStorage), synthesized sound effects (`SPG.sfx`, WebAudio, no files), soft music, UI helpers (`SPG.ui.press` = act on touch-down, `SPG.ui.counter`, `SPG.ui.dpr`), and safe mode (`SPG.safe`).
- `js/voice.js`: every spoken line (`LINES`), critter noises (`SOUNDS`), two voice sets (male/female) from tablet recordings (IndexedDB) or files in `audio/voice/<set>/` listed in `audio/voice/manifest.json`, built-in speech as fallback, per-line mute, praise frequency.
- `js/studio.js`: the grown-ups "Voices" screen (record, hear, delete, mute, choose voice, cheering frequency).
- `js/art.js`, `js/art-garden.js`, `js/art-pets.js`: all illustration (faces, scenery, fruit, plants, friends, avatars, bucket, shovel/can/hand, cats/dogs/parachutes, firefighters, safety net). Shared so the look stays consistent. Scene sky/hills are cached in offscreen canvases.
- `js/glyphs.js`: handwriting-style letters as ordered SVG strokes. Draws cards and drives the tracing game.
- `js/app.js`: players, hub (games look like small app icons, a square picture with a short name and no play button, eight to a page (`PER_PAGE`; 4x2 on landscape, 3x3 on portrait) in a swipeable strip with big arrows and dots (the arrows move to the sides on short landscape phones); each game's `icon(c,w,h)` must look good in a square; a card opens on a short tap so a swipe never starts a game; the Pet Shop storefront stays at the bottom; the page is remembered when returning from a game), parent gate, grown-ups panel (voices, play-time limit, safe mode, players), game start/stop, pause on overlays/hidden tab.
- `games/color-pictures.js` + `games/color-seasons.js` (Coloring Book picture data: 20 everyday + 30 seasonal) and `games/color.js` (its engine, editor and gallery).
- `js/scenery.js`: eight scenery themes (meadow, sunset, night, autumn, snow, beach, farm, city) with far-away things and weather; `SPG.scenery.draw/fader`. Fire Rescue, Rain Bucket, Puzzle Pond, Fruit Splash, Grow a Garden and Bunny Band change place as she plays.
- `js/people.js`, `js/people-wear.js`, `games/style.js`: the Style Studio (see below). `people.js` draws human children from a small "look" object; `people-wear.js` holds the wardrobe catalogs (`P.CATS`), hooks and the big nail-salon hands.
- `js/pets.js` (pet and hat art, saved pets, the companion that sits beside the drawing) and `games/petshop.js` (the Pet Shop screen).
- `js/sync.js` + `worker/index.js`: optional encrypted cloud backup (see Saving and backups).
- `games/*.js`: one file per game. `SPG.games.push({ id, name, order, icon(c,w,h), create(host) })`; `create` returns `{ start, pause, resume, resize, destroy }`. Add tints for a new game id in `tints` in `js/app.js`.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: installable, works offline (network first). `_headers`: Cloudflare security headers (CSP, no framing, microphone only for this site).

## Games
- **Letter Garden** (six activities): trace, flash cards, find the letter (whole alphabet, capital/lowercase mixed, avoids recent repeats), write her name, sounds (pick the picture that starts with the sound), match (pair big and little letters).
- **Fruit Splash**: swipe or tap, up to five fingers at once, no losing (default), rare golden star, lifetime fruit counter, glowing bonus fruit (5 points + a star, rainbow aura), five themes picked from a palette button (classic, neon, candy, space, ocean; `THEMES`, saved as `bag.theme`). Grown-ups > "Fruit Splash age" (`settings.fruitAge`): 'big' adds sly water balloons (`balloon()`); popping one shakes the screen, sprays water and subtracts `BALLOON_COST` (5) fruit points from `bag.total` (never stars).
- **Rain Bucket**: catch drops, rainbow every 8 drops, a meadow of kept flowers, drops counter. Every third rainbow triggers **Raining Cats and Dogs**: a fire truck drives in, two firefighter friends hop out and hold a safety net she drags, pets float down on parachutes, bounce, and walk off calmly (misses land softly too); when done the crew folds the net (`P.spread` 1 -> 0), climbs on the truck and drives off together; saved pets are counted and cats/dogs then visit the garden. The rainbow before that (flowers % 3 === 2) brings a gentle **storm**: three more clouds roll in, soft lightning (a pale flash and a thin bolt) and low thunder (`sfx.zap`, `sfx.thunder`), about nine times as many drops; it lasts ~25 s, then the sun comes out (+1 star). See `startStorm`/`updateStorm` in `games/rain.js`.
- **Grow a Garden**: guided planting steps shown by a step strip: dig three times with the shovel (hole grows), drop in a seed, pat the dirt once, then water with a can she grabs and pours (assisted; or switch to tap mode by tapping the can button again). Blooms unlock seeds (pumpkin 3, carrot 6, lavender 10, rose 15) and a bigger garden (8 blooms). 14 friends to meet (some need rainbows or rescued pets), a friends book, and a "say goodbye" tool to send friends off screen.
- **Fire Rescue** (`games/firerescue.js`): three colorful buildings with friendly flames in some windows and on the roof, and a cat or dog in one window each. She drags a finger to spray the hose (bear firefighter with the fire truck at the side) and the flames shrink and puff into steam; when a building is out its pet bounces with a star, a tap (or ~5 s) sends the fox firefighter running with a ladder, the pet climbs down and sits in front of the building. Three rescued = rainbow, confetti, +1 star (+1 star per building while playing). Nothing bad happens: if nobody plays, the flames fade by themselves (`IDLE_FAST`, `FADE_RATE`), the pets are helped down anyway, no stars are given, and the game rests on a calm sunny scene until the next touch. Tuning constants (`WET_RATE`, `DRY_RATE`, `FADE_RATE`) are at the top. Reuses `art.firefighter`, `art.fireTruck`, `art.pet`.
- **Coloring Book** (`games/color.js`): 50 pictures in five collections shown as tabs with a colored mini picture each (Animal friends, Garden, Halloween, Thanksgiving and fall, Christmas); the season that is coming up opens first (`seasonNow`). Every separate shape (each ear, raindrop, wing, star) is its own colorable section. Each collection has its own instrumental music (`THEMES` in `js/core.js`). The child's pet, if she has one, sits beside the page and reacts. It has a gallery and a painting screen. Bucket fill, brush (paint is clipped to the section where the stroke began, and never crosses into a section in front of it), eraser (drag to rub out, tap to clear a section), multi-step undo, start over (asks first, and can itself be undone), 14 colors, Done (golden frame in the gallery, +1 star the first time; finishing is never a lock). A child saves a picture one at a time as a PNG (the app shows its own "Saved!"; on iPhone/iPad this goes through the grown-up gate). There is no voice at all inside the Coloring Book (`SPG.voice.hushed`) and it has its own gentle music-box music, on by default with its own Grown-ups switch (`settings.colorMusic`).
- **Puzzle Pond** (`games/puzzle.js`): jigsaw puzzles made from her own Coloring Book pictures (`SPG.coloring.draw` renders a picture with her colors, or a bright sample if unpainted; 80% of the time it picks one she has painted). 2x2, then 3x2, 3x3, 4x3 as she solves them. Drag a piece near its place to click it in; the right place glows while a piece is held; a wrong drop just stays put. Piece shapes are real jigsaw knobs (`edge`, `tracePath`). +1 star per puzzle.
- **Pet Care** (`games/petcare.js`): her active pet (or a visiting bunny) in a cozy room. Drag fruit to its mouth (feed), scrub it with the sponge (bath), or tap the moon (bed; night, lullaby, it walks to bed). A thought bubble shows the icon of what it wants. Needs live in `store.bag('care')` and creep back up gently over real time (never past .85). Fed + washed + slept = +1 star. Once asleep the pet stays asleep until she wakes it (room goes dark, night light in three colors, stars in the morning). A Decorate tray adds wallpaper and furniture. Tapping the pet zooms to a close-up for stroking with hand, brush or spray bottle; it purrs (`sfx.purr`, or a recorded `purr/<species>` clip). Fur detail (`detail` option in `SPG.pets.draw`) is used everywhere pets are drawn.
- **Bunny Band** (`games/band.js`): six friends (bunny drum, bear horn, cat xylophone, fox flute, frog bell, panda piano; `sfx.instrument`). Touch to play. Green button = Copy me (a 2-5 note tune, wrong notes just replay it, every second win = +1 star). Red button = record up to 10 s (16 notes) then it loops; touch again to stop.
- **Choo-Choo Train** (`games/train.js`): a train with three cars pulls in; each car's sign asks for a color, a shape or a count (levels grow with trains completed). Drag blocks from the platform into cars; numbers/colors/shapes are spoken (`num/n`, `color/x`, `shape/x` lines); wrong ones bounce home. `ensureSupply` keeps every car solvable. Full train leaves, +1 star. A full car spits an extra block back to the platform (`spitOut`); car colors match the requested color.
- **Hide and Seek** (`games/hide.js`): 1-3 friends (or her own pet) hide behind six garden places. Touching an empty place shakes it and glows blue/yellow/orange (cold/warmer/hot) with a rising chirp. After 12 idle seconds a hider's ears peek out. All found = rainbow and +1 star. The game is now a scrollable world map (`WORLDS`) she walks around in, with a minimap and a hint arrow after a while.
- **Style Studio** (`games/style.js`, hub page 1): dress-up for eight preset children (four girls, four boys, all skin tones) with 11 picture tabs: who, hair (22 styles + salon tools comb/dryer/sparkle spray/bubble wash), make-up (lips, eyeshadow, blush, freckles, face gems), princess dresses (ball gown, party, tutu, mermaid, flower fairy, sundress), tops, skirts/pants, shoes (glass slippers...), crowns and hats, extras (glasses, necklaces, fairy/butterfly/angel wings, capes, wands, balloons...), nail salon (two big hands, 16 polishes, glitter, nail art decals, drag across nails) and places (backdrops). A look is a plain object (`P.defaultLook`); each child keeps their own in `store.bag('style')`. Every tab and item name is spoken (`style/<key>` lines in `STYLE_NAMES` in js/voice.js). The dice button dresses her randomly; the rosette button sends her down a runway (+1 star when the look changed since the last show). To add clothes: add a draw function to the matching table in `people-wear.js` and an entry in `P.CATS`, then add its spoken name to `STYLE_NAMES`. About 100 items in all (more are added by the `add(...)` blocks in people-wear.js). Note `art.shade` only takes hex colors; the people files have their own `shade` that also takes `rgb()`.
- **Pet Shop** (`games/petshop.js`, art and saving in `js/pets.js`): a storefront on the hub (not a game card: `shop: true` in its registration). Stars buy a pet (bunny, kitten, bear, fox, frog, panda; 10-30 stars) and hats (5-20); every purchase asks first, a pet gets a name (dice button picks one), one pet at a time is the companion (beside the drawing in the Coloring Book and walking along the front of the bed in Grow a Garden, cheering on every bloom; tap it for its noise). No real-money purchases exist anywhere. **Names are picked, not typed**: a grid of name buttons (`SPG.ui.nameGrid`) says each name out loud when touched, and the dice picks one; the keyboard field only appears after the grown-up maths gate (`SPG.app.askGate`). Player setup works the same way (a grid of nicknames, plus a gated 'Grown-up: type a name' button). Prices live in `PETS` and `HATS`.
- Garden extras: carrots unlock at 2 blooms and always bring a hungry bunny that walks to a ready carrot or strawberry, nibbles it and the plant is removed (`updateBunny`); the "say goodbye" tool asks first with a soft pink Bye-bye and a green Stay (`askGoodbye`).
Progress and stars are saved per player in localStorage under `spg.v1`.

## Tuning notes
Tracing tolerances are constants at the top of the Tracer section in `games/letters.js` (`TOLERANCE`, `START_RADIUS`, `LOOKAHEAD`); progress follows the nearest path point and cannot outrun the finger. Watering effort is `NEED_DROPS` and the drop rate (`cn.emit`) in `games/garden.js`. Kid-facing buttons use `SPG.ui.press` (touch-down); anything needing a completed tap (fullscreen, first sound) stays on `click`.

## Phones, Safari and Google Play
- Sizes scale with the screen (`--ui`, `--chip`, `--arrow`, `--edge` CSS variables from `vmin`); landscape phones (<520px tall) move the big arrows to the sides and compact the gate/setup. Game canvases size themselves from the canvas rect (bucket, clouds, crew and pets scale with the screen). Check new screens at 390x844, 844x390, 800x1280 and 1280x800.
- Safari: `webkit` fullscreen API is used where it exists (iPad); iPhone has none, so grown-ups are shown the Add to Home Screen steps (Grown-ups panel and a hint on the who-screen) plus Guided Access notes.
- Google Play: the plan is a Trusted Web Activity wrapper. Everything needed is in `store/` (listing text, `PLAY-STORE-GUIDE.md`, Bubblewrap/asset-links templates, generated graphics), plus `privacy.html`, `.well-known/assetlinks.json` (empty until the app is signed), and the manifest. `SPG.config.recorder` (js/core.js) removes all microphone use; `SPG.version` shows in the Grown-ups About section. Regenerate store graphics with `node tools/make-store-assets.mjs` (needs Playwright). Fill the two placeholders in `privacy.html` before publishing.
- Garden animals make tiny, one-at-a-time sounds (`sfx.critter`, or a recorded `critter/<kind>` clip played very quietly by `voice.ambient`).

## Coloring Book: how it works and how to add pictures
- What a child has done is saved per player (`store.bag('color')`) as data, not pixels: an ordered list of operations per picture in the picture's own 1000 x 800 coordinates (see the comment at the top of `games/color.js`). Undo removes the last operation and redraws. The list is compacted on save (anything a later fill of the same section covers is dropped). Colors are saved as positions in `PALETTE`, so only ever append to that list.
- Rendering uses two stacked canvases: paint underneath, outlines and fixed details on top. Sections are Path2D shapes listed back to front; each paint operation is clipped to its section minus the sections in front of it, so shapes may overlap freely as long as no single section crosses itself.
- **Add a picture:** append an object to `PICTURES` in `games/color-pictures.js` (everyday) or `P1`-`P3` style lists in `games/color-seasons.js` (set `group` to a collection id from `SPG.pictureGroups`; to add a collection, add an entry there with a `theme` for its music) (`{ id, name, sections: [{ id, d, det? }] }`, sections back to front, each `d` a closed path ending in `Z`, all inside 1000 x 800; the helper functions at the top build ellipses, blobs, leaves and so on). Then run `node tools/check-pictures.mjs`: it fails on duplicate ids, unclosed paths, self-crossing shapes and shapes too small to tap (44 units), and warns on small shapes or 6-15 section guidance. Never rename or delete a section id that has shipped, or children's saved colors for it are lost. The gallery, hub card and saving pick the picture up automatically.
- Downloads: a hidden link starts a normal file download and the app shows its own "Saved!". A web page cannot switch off the browser's or Android's own download notice.

## Night mode
`SPG.night` (js/core.js): `settings.night` is 'off', 'on' or 'auto' (between `settings.nightFrom` and `settings.nightTo`, chosen in Grown-ups, default 7 pm to 7 am). It toggles `body.night`. Effects: a navy veil (`#night-veil`) over everything, a night-sky backdrop with stars and moon behind the menus, night scenery in every game that draws scenery (`SPG.scenery.draw` forces the night theme, `art.scene` uses a night sky when no sky is given), and master volume .6 instead of .85. A moon button on the hub toggles it; Grown-ups > Night mode sets Day/Night/Auto, and Auto has start and end hour pickers. The moon button, when Auto is chosen, only overrides for the current session (`SPG.night.override`) so the grown-ups' schedule is kept. Style Studio at night hangs a lamp over her (`lampLight` in games/style.js): the stage darkens, a warm cone lights her and a pool of floor, and she is drawn afterwards at full brightness; while it is open `body.st-open` makes the global veil step aside (the panel is dimmed by CSS instead). New games that draw their own sky should check `SPG.night.on()`.

## Safe mode (what it is and is not)
A website cannot lock a device. Safe mode = fullscreen (or installed PWA), no external links anywhere, CSP that blocks any outside load, back button stays in the app, fullscreen-lost overlay, and every way out is behind the parent gate (spelled-out addition). The real lock is Android screen pinning, explained in the grown-ups panel.

## Grown-ups panel features
A **grown-up PIN** (4 numbers, stored as a SHA-256 hash in `settings.pin`) can be set in the panel: once set it replaces the spelled-out sum in the gate everywhere (`askGate` -> `askPin`), including unlocking the rest screen. 'Forgot the PIN?' offers two hard sums in a row that clear it, so nobody is locked out. The **rest screen** (daily limit reached) shows the pets she owns dancing under the moon with a soft music-box lullaby (`SPG.music.rest`, plays even when normal music is off, respects the sound switch).
Voice/sound/soft-music toggles (music is generative WebAudio, off by default), a daily play-time limit (15/30/45/60 min or none) that shows a full-screen rest screen only a grown-up can dismiss (usage is tracked per day in `settings.playLog`), player management, and screen-pinning instructions.

## Voice
Every spoken prompt is a key in `LINES` (`js/voice.js`); critter noises are sound-only keys in `SOUNDS`. Recordings made in the studio live on that tablet; file recordings go in `audio/voice/male|female/<key with / as ->.mp3` and then run `node tools/build-voice-manifest.mjs`. `RECORDING.md` lists every line; regenerate it with `node tools/make-recording-list.mjs` after editing `LINES` or `SOUNDS`.

## Testing notes
Test scripts that need to type a player name must go through the gate first (click `#type-name`, solve the sum, then fill `#name-input`). Hub cards live on pages of four, so select them by name (`.card[aria-label="Fire Rescue"]`), never by `nth-child`.
Verified with headless Chromium (Coloring Book: fill, clipped brush, eraser, undo, start over, Done and frame, persistence, per-player saves, palm and multi-touch, PNG saving, silence, music switch, phone and tablet layouts, 4x CPU throttle) (tablet landscape and portrait, fake microphone): tracing every letter with simulated fingers (careful/fast/wobbly must complete; 14.5+ units off the line must not), all games, guided planting, the cats-and-dogs event played through, five-finger slicing, touch-down presses without double firing, voice recording/playback/mute, gate, back-button trapping, fullscreen-lost overlay, offline reload. Not yet tested on the real Android tablet beyond the owner's play tests: real microphone, speech voice quality, install prompt, screen pinning.

## Deploying
Cloudflare Workers serves the repo root as static assets via `wrangler.jsonc`; `.assetsignore` keeps handoff/tools/config out of the public site. Build command: `npx wrangler deploy`. Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` if you ever need to force-clear old offline caches.

## Name and release tooling
The app is called **Little Sprout Park** (launcher/short name "Sprout Park"). The Cloudflare worker and repo are still named `sunny-play-garden` on purpose; renaming them is the owner's call.
- `node tools/check-release.mjs`: pre-flight for the Google Play release (manifest, icons, offline file list, CSP, privacy placeholders, assetlinks). FAIL = fix in code; WARN = owner to-dos (contact details, signing fingerprint, recorder switch).
- `node tools/make-assetlinks.mjs <package.id> <SHA-256>`: writes `.well-known/assetlinks.json` once Play App Signing gives the fingerprint.
- `node tools/make-icons.mjs`: renders PNG icons from `icons/*.svg`. `node tools/make-store-assets.mjs`: store screenshots and feature graphic. Both need Playwright.
- `store/PLAY-STORE-GUIDE.md` is the owner's step-by-step for the Play Console side.

## Saving and backups
- Data lives in the browser's `localStorage` (`spg.v1`, per device; not cookies, no server). `SPG.store` stamps the active player on every save (`t`), asks the browser for persistent storage, flags a failed save (`store.saveFailed`, shown in Grown-ups), keeps removed/reset players in `store.trash` (Grown-ups > Bring back), and keeps one undo copy before any restore (`store.snapshot`, `undoRestore`).
- `js/sync.js` + `worker/index.js` + `wrangler.jsonc`: optional encrypted cloud backup on Cloudflare (Durable Object, no KV/IDs to set up). Family code -> id + AES-GCM key, so the server cannot read anything. Merge rules are in `mergeProfile` (`js/core.js`): newer player wins, coloring pictures merge per picture, owned pets/hats are unioned; deletions never propagate. Backup file save/restore is in the same Grown-ups section. Full notes and deploy steps: `store/CLOUD-SETUP.md`. The service worker never caches `/api/`. Test locally with `npx wrangler dev` (see that file).

## Guidelines
Keep play relaxed and unlimited, no losing, no reading required, big touch targets. Don't repeat game mechanics across games. Add new games through the `SPG.games` list.
