Privacy Policy

Little Sprout Park · Last updated 30 September 2026

In short: Little Sprout Park collects no personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything a child or grown-up creates in the app stays on their own device, unless a grown-up chooses to turn on the optional private cloud backup described below.

Who we are

Little Sprout Park ("the app") is a free game collection for young children, published by [Developer or company name]. Questions about this policy: [contact email address].

What is stored on the device

To let children keep playing where they left off, the app saves the following only on the device it runs on (in the browser or app storage):

  • Player names and the animal picture a grown-up chooses for each player.
  • Game progress: stars, letters practised, garden plants, friends met, coloring pictures, pets and their names, and similar counters.
  • Coloring Book pictures: which colors were used where. A child can save a picture as an image file to their own device; the app never uploads it.
  • Settings chosen by a grown-up (sound, voice, daily play-time limit, and so on).
  • Optional voice recordings that a grown-up records in the Grown-ups → Voices screen.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, unless a grown-up turns on the optional cloud backup below.

Optional cloud backup (grown-ups only)

A grown-up can turn on a backup so that players, stars and drawings can be brought back if a device is lost or replaced. It is off by default and is controlled from the Grown-ups screen, which is protected by a grown-up check.

  • The app makes a random family code. There is no email address, password or account.
  • Everything is encrypted on the device using a key made from the family code before it is sent. The code itself never leaves the device. We store only the encrypted data and an identifier made from the code, so we cannot read names, progress or drawings, and we cannot recover a lost code.
  • The encrypted copy is stored with Cloudflare, Inc., our hosting provider. We do not use it for anything else, and never for advertising or analytics.
  • A grown-up can delete the cloud copy at any time (Grown-ups → Turn off and delete cloud copy). A backup that has not been used for about 13 months is deleted automatically.

A grown-up can also save a backup file to the device and restore it later. That file is kept only where the grown-up saves it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when a grown-up taps Record in the Grown-ups → Voices screen, so the games can play their voice instead of the device's built-in voice. The recordings are stored only on that device, are never uploaded, and can be deleted at any time from the same screen.

Children

The app is designed for young children to use with a grown-up nearby. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Settings, adding or removing players, and anything that could leave the app are behind a grown-up check. The app contains no links to other websites, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Third parties

The app uses no advertising networks, analytics, social media, or other third-party software that collects data. Fonts and images are included with the app.

How the app is delivered

The app's files are delivered over the internet by our web host (Cloudflare). Like any website, the host may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, time, and browser type for security and to deliver the files. We do not use these logs to identify players. After the first load the app works offline.

Deleting information

A grown-up can remove a player or reset progress in Grown-ups → Players. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, removes everything stored on the device.
